Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience?
No, our bootcamp is designed for beginners. We'll teach you everything you need to know from the ground up.
What equipment do I need?
You'll need a laptop with internet access. All the software we use is free and web-based.
How much time should I commit per week?
We recommend at least 15-20 hours per week for the best results, including classes and independent practice.
What happens after the bootcamp?
Successful participants will be invited to join our full Codetribe program, which offers extended learning and job placement assistance.
